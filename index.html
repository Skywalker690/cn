A little context for your connection.

Four numbers. Everyday impact.

01 / Download speed

How quickly data reaches your device. Faster downloads help websites load, videos stream smoothly, and large files arrive sooner.

02 / Upload speed

How quickly you send data. More upload speed helps with sharing files, cloud backups, and sending clear video in meetings.

03 / Ping

A round-trip delay to a server while the connection is idle. Lower ping helps online games and conversations feel responsive.

04 / Loaded latency

Ping is a latency measurement. Here, latency shows the delay during a download, helping explain lag when your network gets busy.
